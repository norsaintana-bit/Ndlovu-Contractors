# Ndlovu Contractors website launch kit

**Launch date:** 15 November 2026  
**Brand:** Ndlovu Contractors Pty Ltd  
**Service area:** Mdantsane, East London and surrounding Eastern Cape

## Launch objective

Use the new website to make Ndlovu Contractors easier to trust, easier to contact and easier to recommend. The first launch message should focus on the range of support available in one place: architectural services, building and renovations, carpentry and interiors, and construction materials supply.

## Simple 7-day launch plan

**Day 1 — Announcement:** Publish the launch post below on Facebook and WhatsApp Status. Use the website link and a clean screenshot of the new homepage.

**Day 2 — Services:** Share one post about architectural services. Show a floor plan, a sketch or a 2D-to-3D visual if the business has an approved example to publish.

**Day 3 — Building work:** Share a short before-and-after, progress image or simple project detail. Keep the caption factual and avoid claims that are not supported by a real project.

**Day 4 — Interiors:** Feature built-in kitchens or wardrobes. Ask followers which room they would improve first.

**Day 5 — Materials:** Remind local builders and homeowners that Ndlovu Contractors can supply bricks, sand, concrete and other construction materials with same-day delivery.

**Day 6 — Local reach:** Post the Mdantsane / East London service area and invite people to send a WhatsApp message for a project conversation.

**Day 7 — Follow-up:** Re-share the launch post with a direct call to action: “Planning a build, renovation or fitted interior? Contact Ndlovu Contractors today.”

Use real project photographs, approved drawings and genuine progress updates whenever possible.
